About FotoFrame

That old iPad in the drawer still has a beautiful screen. FotoFrame gives it a second life as a digital photo frame: a calm, full-screen slideshow of your favourite photos that crossfades from one picture to the next.

Show photos from your device, or pick them from Google Photos. You don't need an account to get started — try the built-in demo slideshow first, or choose photos from the library on your device.

Features

  • Photos from your devicePick photos from the library on your iPad, iPhone or Android device. No login needed.
  • Google PhotosChoose photos with Google's own picker. FotoFrame only sees the photos you select, never your whole library.
  • Built for an always-on frameStays awake, loops forever, resumes by itself when you reopen the app, and keeps playing through short connection drops.
  • Your paceChoose how long each photo stays (5 seconds to 1 minute), shuffle on or off, swipe to skip, tap to pause.
  • Portrait photos look goodPhotos that don't fill the screen get a soft blurred background instead of black bars.
  • Private by designNo account with us, no ads, no analytics. Your photos never leave your device and Google.

Set up your frame

On iPad and iPhone

  • Keep the screen onSettings → Display & Brightness → Auto-Lock → Never. Keep the iPad plugged in.
  • Lock it to FotoFrameSettings → Accessibility → Guided Access → On. Then, with the slideshow playing, triple-click the side or Home button and tap Start. This stops accidental taps from leaving the app.
  • After a restart or power cutGuided Access has to be started again by hand. Open FotoFrame and it picks up your slideshow by itself.

On Android

Set the screen timeout to the longest setting, keep the device plugged in, and use Screen pinning (Settings → Security) to lock it to FotoFrame.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a Google account?
No. You can use photos from your device or the demo slideshow. A Google account is only needed if you want to show photos from Google Photos.
Does FotoFrame upload or store my photos?
No. We don't run any servers. Photos from Google Photos are streamed from Google to your device, and photos from your device stay on your device. See the privacy policy.
Why can't it start Guided Access by itself?
Apple only allows Guided Access to be started by the user (or by a managed-device administrator). That is why it needs a triple-click after every restart.
My Google Photos selection stopped working.
Google Photos selections are time-limited by Google. If yours has expired, FotoFrame says so; open the app and choose your photos again. Photos from your device never expire.
Can I mix Google Photos and device photos?
Not in the same slideshow. Use "New Selection" to switch between the two.
Which devices does it run on?
iPad and iPhone (iPadOS/iOS), and Android phones and tablets.
