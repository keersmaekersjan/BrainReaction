About Bridge Beasts

A storm has wrecked your balloon and shattered the bridges between the jungle islands, and the animals have carried off the planks. A small brain creature called Bram needs your help to put the bridges back together.

Bridge Beasts trains bridging over ten: adding and subtracting across a tens boundary by splitting the sum in two steps (8 + 5 = 8 + 2 + 3, or 13 − 5 = 13 − 3 − 2). Every correct answer lays a plank in the bridge, and a quick answer lays two. When a beast has handed over all its planks it calms down and becomes your friend, and the path to the next island opens. A Practice Room under the crashed balloon lets kids drill without a timer, with a hint that shows the split-up sum when they get stuck.

Why kids and parents like it

  • Learn the method, then make it automaticThe question starts with the bridge visible and gradually asks for it from memory, with growing speed.
  • Choose adding, subtracting or bothThe steps 2 to 9 are always in play; parents pick the operation.
  • Five islands, 25 beastsEach island has four beasts and a boss. Every beast you beat becomes a friend in your collection.
  • Practice RoomEndless, untimed practice with a streak counter and a hint after a few seconds.
  • Progress for parentsA statistics screen shows the average answering speed for adding and subtracting. No account needed.
  • Up to five childrenEach child has their own profile and progress on the same device.
