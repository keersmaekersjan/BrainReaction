About Multiplication Monsters

While your child was asleep, mischievous monsters snuck in and stole their toys! The only way to win them back is by defeating the monsters — one times table at a time.

Multiplication Monsters replaces repetitive multiplication drills with battle-style gameplay. Kids pick which times tables they want to practice, answer multiplication questions to defeat monsters, and unlock new magical worlds as they go. A dedicated Practice Room lets them drill specific tables at their own pace, without a timer, whenever they just want to warm up.

In the Netherlands and Belgium, the app is also listed as Maaltafel Monsters for Dutch-speaking families.

It's designed for kids roughly ages 6 to 12 who are learning or reviewing their multiplication tables (PEGI 3).

Yes — on the Dutch-language Play Store it's listed as "Maaltafel Monsters".
What platforms does it run on?
Multiplication Monsters is available for Android phones and tablets via Google Play.
